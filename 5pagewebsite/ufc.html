Why My Favorite Promotion is the UFC

My favorite promotion is the UFC because it has most of the best fighters in the world. It has many of the best fights in the history of MMA(for example Robbie Lawler vs Rory MacDonald 2). Most of the fights are entertaining but if you aren't a fan of wrestling/grappling most of the Dagestani fighters will be seen as boring to you. Another reason is because of the great Bruce Buffer with his amazing announcer voice. These are the main reasons I enjoy watching the UFC
