What is MMA?

MMA, also known as Mixed Martial Arts, is a combat sport that allows fighters to use many different styles and techniques of fighting.

Key Elemts of MMA

  • Striking: Punches, kicks, knees, and elbows
  • Grappling: Wrestling, jiu jitsu, and clinches
  • Submissions: Chokes and locks on joints

Main martial arts used

  • Boxing for striking
  • Kick Boxing for striking
  • Muay Thai for striking and clinches
  • Wrestling for takedowns and some ground control
  • Judo for throws
  • Brazilian Jiu Jitsu for most ground control and submissions

How fights end and rules

How fights end

Fights can end in 3 different ways

  • Decision: Judges score the fights, normally using a 10 point must system
  • KO/TKO: Fight ends due to one of the fighters egtting knocked out or they are no longer defending themselves intelligently. Though a TKO can also happen in the medical doctor ends it due to injuries like serious cuts, if someone breaks a limb or other injuries, if the fighters corner stops it, and if the fighter decides they no longer want to continue.
  • Submission: If a fighter taps, goes unconscious, or breaks something from a choke or lock

Rules

Many MMA promotions have different rules for example in the UFC you can not stomp or soccer kick a downed opponent but in other promotions like PRIDE FC or Rizin you can.

So over all rules just depend on the MMA pronmotion.
